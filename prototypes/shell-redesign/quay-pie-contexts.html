Quay Pie Contexts

What the two-ring pie holds when it opens over a terminal, over the companion's pull request, over the module bar or a panel row, and over an empty stage. Three schemes, same neon wireframe.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a scheme with the three buttons below.
  2. Point at any wedge in the four pies. Its second ring lights up and its name shows under the pie.
  3. Read the grid at the bottom: green rows are strokes that never change, so they become habit.
  4. Then answer the questions back in the terminal.

    From the pointer research

    Each direction keeps one command or family in every context. Most-used commands on up, down, left and right. A command that does not apply leaves its slot empty.

    From the right-click decision

    Plain right-click in a terminal already copies and pastes, so the terminal pie does not need Copy or Paste.

    Blind strokes

    Experts make close to one mistake in ten at eight by two. Merge and Close on a flick need a guard: a second ring, a confirm, or an undo window.
