What today's code holds

Quay is dark-only. Colours are written straight into Tailwind classes: about 4,000 hex colour classes across 111 files in src on master today. The blue accent alone is about 370 of them.

Why the cost differs

B only needs the accent to become one token the pie palette can set, plus glow on five parts. C and D change every surface, so every colour class has to move to tokens first.

Reading dense text

Every look clears 4.5:1 for muted text on its panel. C is the darkest, so its ratios are the highest on paper. What the ratios miss is the glow and grid lines C puts around every list, diff and terminal.

My recommendation: B · Neon accents

  • The pie stops looking like a visitor: the same colours mark the open module, the selected terminal, status and focus.
  • Dense text stays on today's surfaces, so terminals, diffs and lists read exactly as they do now.
  • Smallest build: one accent token and glow on five parts, against moving about 4,000 colour classes for C or D.
