Quay Builder Canvas

The Praxis Builder opens as a stage tab. Today's Builder has its own definitions list (224 px) and inspector (288 px), so between the module panel and the companion its canvas shrinks to about 420 px on a 1600 px window. Four ways to give the canvas room, and how each brings the rest back. The card below walks you through it.

        My recommendation

        B · Inspector in the companion

        • The companion column's job is "detail for what is in front of you". With a terminal in front that is its pull request; with the Builder in front it is the selected node. So the inspector moves into that column, and the Builder no longer pays for two right-hand columns.
        • The module panel folds when the Builder tab comes to the front, and comes back by itself when you switch to any other tab. The Workflows icon still opens it at any time, and the module bar's badges stay visible, so nothing waiting on you is hidden.
        • The definitions list becomes a 40 px rail; its button opens the list over the canvas and closes it once you pick one.
        • That gives the widest canvas of the four while staying inside the shell: about 1,170 px on a 1600 px window, against about 420 px today.

        The cost: while the Builder is in front, the companion cannot show a pull request. In my own run of the job, following the outline, B took the most clicks: 8, against 7 for A and D and 6 for C. The extra one opens the definitions rail. I judge the wider canvas worth one click.
