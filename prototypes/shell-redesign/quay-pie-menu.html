Why a pie menu is fast

Direction, not distance

Each action owns a wedge that runs outward without end. A 40 px flick and a 400 px throw both land, so there is no small target to slow down for. The readout shows how short your strokes get.

It opens where the pointer is

Travel to the menu is zero. Compare that with crossing the window to a toolbar button and back.

The stroke becomes a habit

Merge is always up. After a few uses you stroke before reading, and the menu is only a confirmation. Eight directions per ring is the usual ceiling for doing that reliably; the research ticket will check that figure against the marking-menu studies.
